- Hero pattern: lowercase two-tone headline (dark first line, gray continuation) + subhead + filled black "download Paper" + arrow text link; product UI screenshot directly below.
- Section sequence: hero > product canvas with terminal/agent demo > two-column feature statements in a ruled grid.

- Color strategy: warm off-white #efefe4 paper, ink #181818, gray #666 for secondary text (4.96:1 body contrast measured).
- Signature detail / effect: Paper Shaders detected (fingerprint), paper texture and a folded-corner grid illustration; ruled lines like a drafting sheet.
- What to borrow: warm paper background with hairline rules and corner ticks; two-tone headline; terminal snippet as proof.
- What to avoid: gray continuation text near the 4.5:1 limit; copying the folded-corner graphic.
