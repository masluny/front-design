## Observations

- Layout and grid: full-width dark canvas, 3-column feature row separated by hairline dividers (desktop-03), sticky header.
- Hero pattern: large H1 (72px) with product UI screenshot below; short subhead.
- Section sequence: hero > product UI > three principles ("Purpose-built", "Powered by agents", "Designed for speed") > feature deep dives (Intake, Planning...).
- Type pairing and voice: Inter Variable with many weights (300-590) + Berkeley Mono labels "FIG 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3" in small caps mono.
- Color strategy: #08090a near-black, layered grays for text hierarchy, accent #5e6ad2 used sparingly.
- Signature detail / effect: isometric line drawings captioned like figures in a paper ("FIG 0.1"); hairline column dividers.
- What to borrow: figure captions in mono ("FIG 1") give a research-paper feel that suits evidence-based copy; hairline dividers instead of cards (P14).
- What to avoid: dark theme; the indigo accent (it would read as the stock AI look).
