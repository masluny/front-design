# PostHog (personality counterpoint)

URL: https://posthog.com

## Observations

- Layout and grid: an OS desktop metaphor: icons on both sides, the homepage is a window; page is one viewport tall (summary.md).
- Hero pattern: bold headline with highlighted phrase ("context layer" in a blue marker), highlighted keywords in body, a setup card with two CTAs and checkmarks.
- Section sequence: hero + setup card > tabbed product demo (Ask PostHog anything).
- Type pairing and voice: custom rounded RoundHog + Source Code Pro; very informal voice.
- Color strategy: warm gray #eeefe9 base, orange CTAs #cd8407 / #eb9d2a, magenta tabs, highlighter marks on text.
- Signature detail / effect: highlighter-pen marks over key phrases; 3D-ish orange buttons with a hard bottom shadow (strong signifier, P7).
- What to borrow: highlighter marks on the exact words that matter (fits a research notebook); tactile buttons with clear affordance.
- What to avoid: the OS metaphor and density (Hick's law load); unverifiable social proof numbers ("500,000+ teams") are their data, we have none.
