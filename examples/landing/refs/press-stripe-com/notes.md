# Stripe Press (wildcard: book publisher)

URL: https://press.stripe.com

## Observations

- Layout and grid: books as 3D spines stacked in the center, tiny serif logo top left, vertical tick index on the left edge.
- Hero pattern: no headline at all; the objects (books) are the hero. Ivar Headline/Ivar Text serif (tokens.json).
- Section sequence: stack of book spines, each opens a book page.
- Type pairing and voice: one serif family in two optical sizes; italic subtitle "Ideas for progress".
- Color strategy: deep warm brown-black #201819 with richly colored book covers (marbled paper, gold).
- Signature detail / effect: three.js book spines; marbled-paper textures; a left-edge tick ruler used as navigation.
- What to borrow: the feeling of a well-made book: serif italic subtitles, marbled/grain texture as the one rich material, a tick ruler motif.
- What to avoid: hiding the value proposition (P1/P2: we are not famous, the tagline must explain us); the measured body contrast (1.21:1 against the page bg) shows text living only on covers.
